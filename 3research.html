Research

Born in Peru, raised in North Carolina.
Working on research projects that co-create solutions
to climate-related problems with local communities and organizations

Oak Woodland and Wildfire in Northern California

Part of my dissertation work is looking at oak woodland responses to wildfire in Napa and Sonoma County forests. This involves looking at physical properties of soil, sequencing soil bacteria and fungi, as well as measuring tree composition.

Nursery experiment in Napa, California

Part of my dissertation work is conducting a nursery experiment in Napa, California, to study the growth and development of oak seedlings under different environmental conditions.

Miyawaki-style reforesting experiment in Northern California

Part of my dissertation work is testing neighbor planting and soil inoculations using the Miyawaki method focused on bulking up the soil microbiome to create self-sustaining and resilient forests that establish within three years.

Diatom bloom in the NES-LTER

As part of my undergraduate studies, I was part of the Stanley Lab at Wellesley College, where we studies productivity in the Northeast US Shelf. We found an unusual diatom bloom that increased the net export of carbon into the ocean during summer 2022.

Surface Microlayer in Delaware Bay

As part of my undergraduate career, I worked in the Wozniak Lab at University of Delaware. There I worked on methods to collect the surface microlayer on a research cruise and studied differences between the surface microlayer and the bulk water in Delaware Bay during summer 2022.
